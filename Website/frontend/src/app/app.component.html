Approved Field Chemical Standards

  • Bacterial Spot: Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L + Streptocycline @ 1 g/10 L.
  • Cercospora Leaf Spot: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L OR Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L.
  • Curl Virus: Do NOT spray fungicides. Manage Whitefly vector with Neem oil (3-5 ml/L) or Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 1.2 g/L.
  • Healthy Leaves: Zero chemicals required. Continue regular scouting.
